I am a Ph.D. student in [Chemical and Biological Engineering](https://cbe.princeton.edu/) at Princeton University, advised by [Prof. Christos Maravelias](https://cbe.princeton.edu/people/christos-maravelias). My research lies at the intersection of **machine learning**, **large-scale optimization**, and **model-based control**, with the goal of building adaptive decision-making tools for complex process systems under uncertainty.

Before Princeton, I received an M.Sc. in Data Science for Chemical Engineering from the University of Minnesota and a B.Eng. in Chemical Engineering and Management from East China University of Science and Technology.
